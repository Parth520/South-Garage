Our Story

Our Mission

At South Garage, we believe cooking should be both an art and a science. Our mission is to make advanced culinary techniques accessible to everyone through the power of machine learning, while preserving the innocence and joy of home cooking.

Our Journey

2015

Founded in a small garage with the idea of combining cooking science with technology.

2017

First prototype of our recipe recommendation algorithm completed.

2019

Launched our first mobile app with basic cooking assistance features.

2021

Integrated machine learning for real-time cooking adjustments and feedback.

2023

Expanded to full web platform with comprehensive cooking assistance suite.
